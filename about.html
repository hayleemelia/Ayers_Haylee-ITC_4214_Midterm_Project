What is a Cat Cafe?

Discover what makes a cat cafe special—a cozy space where you can enjoy drinks and snacks while spending time with friendly cats. Learn more about our mission and values to see how we create a safe, welcoming environment for both cats and visitors in Athens.

Our Mission

The mission of the cat cafe is to offer the ultimate cat experience, where every moment is infused with warmth, joy and the soothing presence of our beloved felines. It is not just a cafe, it is a meeting point for all cat lovers where they can socialize, spend time with the kitties and make a difference in their lives. We become the voice of our beloved pets by informing the general public about their needs, responsible adoption, their guardianship and to care for the well-being of as many stray cats as we can. Join us in creating a community where every cat finds comfort, care, a forever home, and at the same time we support those who support our beloved pets.

Our Values

Animal Welfare: Concern for the health, safety and well-being of cats as well as for the promotion of the lawful, conscientious guardianship and care of our little friends.

Compassion: A culture of kindness, empathy, and respect for both guests and cats.

Innovation: Commitment to finding new and creative ways to enhance the cat cafe experience, such as offering unique cat-related products or events.

Education: The cat is a much misunderstood animal, haunted by superstitions from the depths of centuries. We want to educate the public about its well-being, temperament, needs and responsible care. In this way, we contribute to raising awareness and promoting positive changes in the community.
