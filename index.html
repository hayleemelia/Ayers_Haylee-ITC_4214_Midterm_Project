Welcome to the Athens Cat Cafe!

Our cat cafe is the first social enterprise concerning cats in Athens. Come for a visit and meet our furry friends while you enjoy a nice coffee or dessert!

Adoptions

One of the main goals of the cat cafe is to contribute to the reduction of stray cats! This is how we bring interested guardians into contact with prospective kittens for adoption. The animal welfare organization carries out the entire process.

Cat Festival 2026

The annual Cat Festival is a 2-day celebration dedicated to those who love cats! The festival brings together cat lovers, artists, pet companies and more, creators and animal welfare organizations with a common goal of caring for, informing and adopting stray cats. Visitors can meet cats for adoption, participate in workshops to support animal welfare initiatives and learn about their actions. It is an experience that combines entertainment, awareness and love for animals, inviting young and old to celebrate with us.

Cat Workshops

Our Athens cat cafe occasionally hosts fun and creative workshops for cat lovers of all ages. From cat-themed art and crafts to interactive sessions on cat care and behavior, there’s something for everyone to enjoy. Join a cozy, hands-on experience where you can learn, create, and spend quality time with our friendly feline residents. Keep an eye on our schedule to catch upcoming workshops and special events!
