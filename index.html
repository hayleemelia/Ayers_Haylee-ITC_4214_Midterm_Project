Welcome to the Athens Cat Cafe!

Our cat cafe is the first social enterprise concerning cats in Athens. Come for a visit and meet our furry friends while you enjoy a nice drink or dessert!

Reservations Only

To ensure the safety of our cats and smooth operation of our business, we only allow those with reservations to enter our cafe. Those who are 14+ can make a reservation using the link below. Other rules also apply.

Book your Spot!

Adoptions

One of the main goals of the cat cafe is to contribute to the reduction of stray cats! This is how we bring interested guardians into contact with prospective kittens for adoption. The animal welfare organization carries out the entire process.
