Rules to Follow

Ensure a safe and enjoyable visit by following our cat-friendly cafe rules, including age limits, spending requirements, and proper interaction with our furry friends. For any questions about the rules, feel free to visit our contact page for guidance.

Cafe Rules

  1. Relaxed Environment: In order to comply with the welfare rules, it is our duty to ensure both the physical and mental health and peace of mind of the cat. We avoid any kind of movements that may be disturbing to the animal, such as screams and sudden noises. In this way, the cats will be able to enjoy a relaxed and quiet environment with us.
  2. Don't Upset Them: Cats are independent animals with character and personality. They are not stuffed animals! For this reason, we try to approach them slowly, attracting their attention and observing their mood. Act accordingly if they want to play with you, be petted or just want you to admire them!
  3. Respect their Sleep: Cats are nocturnal animals and need to rest during the day with long naps. You can admire them during the adoption day, without disturbing their rest with petting or playing and keeping your voice low.
  4. Don't Feed Them: Don't give in and don't trust their pleas! They always have full bellies and high-quality food at their disposal. We are aware that some foods, which seem "innocent", such as onions or chocolate, are toxic to cats and can lead to their death. Don't use your food to get their attention, but your tricks!
  5. Games: Cats love to play, but only with the toys available in our store. Please do not bring toys or use your hands. This will make them feel comfortable and prevent them from giving you a few unexpected little scratches!
  6. Other Pets: Cats on adoption day want their own personal space and do not accept the intrusion of other animals. Therefore, for their smooth living, you are not allowed to enter the store with any pet you wish to bring (cat, dog or other animals)
  7. Hygiene and Cleanliness: The premises are cleaned daily, morning and evening, thus ensuring an always fresh and clean environment. Upon entering our store, hand washing is mandatory.

Additional Info

Before your visit, please also note that the minimum spending per person is 7€ and we only allow those who are 14+ to enter our cafe to ensure a safe environment for our cats.
